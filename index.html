On the coast, swim only inside stinger nets at patrolled beaches between the flags (nets stop box jellyfish but not tiny Irukandji). The free Esplanade Lagoon in Cairns is the easy swim. source · source

Melbourne days (Thu Nov 5 to Sat Nov 7)

Getting around

  • Free Tram Zone covers the CBD grid and Docklands, including Queen Vic Market. City Circle tram 35 loops the edge. map
  • St Kilda (trams 12, 16, 96) and Brighton (Sandringham line train) are outside the free zone, so you need myki.
  • SkyBus airport ↔ Southern Cross A$25.90 one way online. SkyBus

Key hours & facts

  • Queen Victoria Market: Tue/Thu/Fri 6am to 3pm, Sat 6am to 4pm, Sun 9am to 4pm; closed Mon & Wed. Thu arrival is too late (closes 3pm), so go Fri or Sat morning. QVM
  • Melbourne Skydeck: from Nov 2, 12pm to 10pm (last entry 9:30pm); adults ~A$33 to 43 online. Skydeck
  • Melbourne Museum: 9am to 5pm daily, adult A$18. Museum
  • NGV International (free general entry), Royal Botanic Gardens (free), Fed Square. NGV · RBG · Fed Square
  • Sunset Fri Nov 6: 7:59pm. timeanddate
🐧 St Kilda penguins: free, but booking is essential. The viewing platform is at the end of the rebuilt St Kilda Pier (reopened Dec 2024). Two sessions nightly, ~30 min and ~1h30 after sunset, ~45 min each. Tickets release every Tuesday at 10am (Melbourne time) for the following week's sessions and go fast. For Nov 5/6, check the Tue Oct 27 release (that's Mon Oct 26 afternoon, PT). No flash, and bring a warm layer. Phillip Island Nature Parks: St Kilda

Thu Nov 5 · arrival afternoon & evening

12:15pm land → SkyBus → check in (~2pm). Laneways loop on foot: Degraves St & Centre Place (coffee), Block Arcade / Royal Arcade, Hosier Lane street art (reopened after paving works; street-art walk), Flinders St Station, Fed Square, a Yarra riverside stroll to Southbank, and Skydeck at sunset (~8pm). Dinner in Chinatown or Flinders Lane.

Fri Nov 6 · full day (city version)

QVM breakfast (hot jam doughnuts) → free tram → NGV International → Royal Botanic Gardens & Shrine of Remembrance → afternoon tram to St Kilda: Acland St cake shops, the beach, Luna Park facade → penguins session ~8:30pm → tram back.

Day-trip version (Fri): Go West Great Ocean Road, A$168 online / A$159 by phone, pickup from selected central hotels ~7 to 8am, back ~8:30 to 9:30pm, Twelve Apostles, Loch Ard Gorge, Gibson Steps. Or Phillip Island full day (A$185 online), or the afternoon Penguins Express (from A$163 online; meets at 400 Flinders St at 2:45pm AEDT; back 9pm to midnight), which keeps your Friday morning for the city. All include Penguin Parade entry where relevant; published prices, check for your dates.

Sat Nov 7 · until the afternoon flight

Option 1: train to Brighton Beach station and walk ~500 m to the Brighton bathing boxes at Dendy St Beach (Bayside Council). Option 2: QVM Saturday (6am to 4pm) + Melbourne Museum & Carlton Gardens. Check out, SkyBus ~2:30pm, Jetstar 4:45pm. Note it's Champions Stakes race day at Flemington, so expect busier trams/trains west of the CBD.
