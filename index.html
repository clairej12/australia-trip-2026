The only boat where the intro (resort) dive is included in the fare. Snorkel at 2 outer-reef sites picked daily (Hastings, Saxon, Norman, Breaking Patches, Jorgensen Patch), max 75 guests, guided intro dives at max 4 divers per instructor, breakfast + BBQ lunch, free CBD hotel bus. Melbourne: Thu Nov 5 afternoon to Sat Nov 7 afternoon

Why Melbourne is worth it: Sydney has the harbour; Melbourne is where Australians go to eat, wander and look at things. The city centre is a walkable grid of laneways, arcades and street art packed with cafés; the big galleries, the State Library and the Botanic Gardens are free; and at dusk wild little penguins come ashore at a city pier. It's also cheap to do without a car: trams inside the CBD are free, and Victoria's public transport is half price for everyone until Jan 1, 2027.

Getting around
